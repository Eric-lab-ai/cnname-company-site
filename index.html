前传 · 五行与姓名PROLOGUE · ELEMENTS & NAMES

五行与姓名：中国人用了几百年的取名密码The five elements & names: China's centuries-old naming code

明朝有件特别中国的事——皇帝给子孙起名，用的正是金木水火土。There's something very Chinese about the Ming: the emperor named his descendants with metal, wood, water, fire, earth.

据明代宗谱记载，明太祖朱元璋给二十多个儿子定下字辈，又立了一道规矩：孙辈取名，偏旁要按「木生火、火生土、土生金、金生水、水生木」一轮轮走。于是翻开明朝宗室谱，会看到一幅奇景——燕王朱棣、周王朱橚、楚王朱桢……兄弟的名字清一色木字旁；他们的儿子朱高炽、朱高煦……齐刷刷火字旁；再下一辈朱瞻基、朱瞻墉……又落回土字旁。一个王朝的姓名，写满了一部流动的五行。这不是故弄玄虚，是古人把对天地运转的理解，安放进最寻常的取名里。从皇家到市井，看五行、求相生、给孩子讨个好彩头，是延续数百年的习惯。Ming clan genealogies record that founding emperor Zhu Yuanzhang set generation names for his twenty-odd sons, then a rule: each generation's name must carry a radical from the cycle—wood → fire → earth → metal → water. Open the Ming royal genealogy and you see something uncanny: Princes Zhu Di, Zhu Su, Zhu Zhen… all wood radicals; their sons Zhu Gaochi, Zhu Gaoxu… all fire; the next, Zhu Zhanji, Zhu Zhanyong… back to earth. An entire dynasty's names, a flowing map of the five elements. Not mystification—the ancients weaving their sense of how the world moves into the most everyday act of naming. From palace to lane, reading the elements and wishing a child well became a habit of centuries.
